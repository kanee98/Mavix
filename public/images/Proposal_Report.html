Executive Overview & Strategic Context

Establishing market leadership for Glarie Elevators and FMT Power Generators in Sri Lanka.

Background & Strategic Objectives

3DH Building Solutions holds exclusive agency rights in Sri Lanka for premier engineering products: Glarie Elevators (Passenger, Home, Freight, Panoramic) and FMT Generators (10–2500 kVA Soundproof Systems), supported by a flagship showroom in Colombo 2 and a 24/7 technical workshop in Kandana. Mavix Digital Marketing has developed a high-frequency tri-lingual social media strategy designed to turn 3DH's digital presence into a primary engine for brand awareness and market reach.
